## Notes that apply to all three

- **Re-normalise every cut** to −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP. Do not rely on the platform.
- **Re-lay the captions** for the shortened timeline; never leave a master caption timecode in a short cut. Build the caption sheet from `generated/caption-timing-*.csv` and re-time by the block, not by hand.
- **No new claims.** A social cut is where a price, a "free shipping" line or an unverified platform name is most likely to be improvised in a caption. Gates A–L apply to these cuts exactly as they do to the master.
- **Keep the frame rate** at the master's. Do not mix 25 and 30 across deliverables — the platform will normalise unpredictably and the motion will judder on the faster cut.
