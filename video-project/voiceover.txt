Raaz Number 5 — Patharon ka Wazan.

Great Pyramid of Giza lakhon stone blocks se bana hai. In mein se bahut se blocks ka wazan kai tons tha.

Sabse hairan karne wali baat ye hai ke ye sab us zamane mein hua jab modern construction machinery maujood nahi thi.

Researchers ka maanna hai ke ancient Egyptians ramps, sledges aur organized teams ki madad se bhaari patharon ko transport karte the.

Lekin construction ki exact technique ke kuch details par aaj bhi research aur debate jaari hai.

Raaz Number 4 — Hairat Angeez Precision.

Great Pyramid sirf bada hi nahi, balki bahut precise bhi hai.

Iski sides cardinal directions—north, south, east aur west—ke saath hairat angez accuracy se aligned hain.

Hazaron saal pehle itni precise measurement hasil karna ancient Egyptian engineering aur astronomy ki advanced understanding ko dikhata hai.

Raaz Number 3 — Andar Chhupe Raaste.

Pyramid bahar se ek massive stone structure nazar aata hai, lekin andar passages aur chambers bhi maujood hain.

Modern technology ne researchers ko bina structure ko damage kiye pyramid ke andar study karne mein madad di hai.

Isi research mein Great Pyramid ke andar kuch previously unknown spaces ke evidence bhi mile hain.

In spaces ka exact purpose samajhne ke liye research abhi bhi jaari hai.

Raaz Number 2 — Pyramid Itna Smooth Tha?

Aaj Great Pyramid rough stone structure jaisa nazar aata hai.

Lekin jab ye naya bana tha, iska bahari hissa smooth white limestone casing se cover tha.
